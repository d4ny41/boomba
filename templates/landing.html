boomba

Boomba pulls the tracks you've been listening to on Spotify, lets you rate them from 1 to 5, and shows how your scores compare with your friends'.

The app runs in Spotify's Development Mode, so logging in only works for a small set of allowlisted test accounts. If you're not on the list, the demo video and source code show how it works.
